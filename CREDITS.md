Game art and sounds

The sprites (ships, tiles, effects, UI) and sound effects under public/assets/ were supplied by the challenge organizers in the repository junglegaming/game-developer-challenge (assets/ folder), as the visual and audio foundation required by the challenge brief.

That repository does not include a license file, nor the name or author of the original asset pack. The assets are therefore used here only for the purpose of this technical challenge, and all rights remain with their original authors. No ownership is claimed over them.

Modifications made by this project: atlas/tilesheet handling and image loading optimizations only; the artwork itself was not redrawn.

If the original author or license of the pack is identified, this file will be updated with the proper attribution.
